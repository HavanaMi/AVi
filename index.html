About the Story

Anna Veyne · Contemporary Women’s Fiction / Drama

Lottie leads a life that appears ordinary and settled from the outside. She works in a library and has a husband, family, friends and routines shaped by everyday responsibilities, conversations and plans. Although she once imagined a different future, she has gradually grown accustomed to the life she has built.

When everything she thought was certain begins to change, Lottie must face not only the loss of the world she knows, but also the question of who she is beyond the roles she has occupied for years.

Unrecognisable is a contemporary story about change, the loss of security and rebuilding a life. It explores how even an apparently ordinary existence can suddenly take a different direction — and how dreams set aside long ago do not always disappear forever.
