Behind Unrecognisable

Why I wrote this story

There is so much happening in the world that can feel difficult or overwhelming. Sometimes we simply need to step away from everyday life for a while. We travel, go on holiday or look for a change of scenery. For me, reading a book can offer that same kind of escape — a chance to leave our surroundings behind and enter another world.

With Unrecognisable, I wanted to tell a story rooted in seemingly ordinary home life. From the outside, everything may look normal, yet that does not mean everyone is happy. Sometimes things begin to fall apart just when we feel most secure. It can happen to any of us.

But we also have dreams, even when it is difficult to believe in a better tomorrow. I wanted this novel to make room for both the difficulties of life and the possibility that dreams can still come true.

I have watched films that I enjoyed until an ending left me disappointed and changed how I felt about the whole story. That experience shaped my approach to this book. I wanted to give readers an emotionally satisfying, hopeful ending — one that would leave them glad they had taken the journey.

    The publishing title and a visual direction for the book cover are developed.

  8. October 2026

  9. Ongoing
