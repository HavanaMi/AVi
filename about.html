About AVi Books

A space for stories

Welcome to AVi, a home for books, imagination and creative expression.

Here you can explore current and upcoming publications, read selected excerpts and discover the ideas behind each book.

Several novels are currently in development. Their working titles and story concepts may evolve as the manuscripts take shape.

Unrecognisable, developed from an earlier manuscript, is the first completed novel. Its publication details will be announced when confirmed.

The books presented here are original creative projects, and the writing journey is still unfolding.

The writing journey

Stories in the making — since 2024

The earliest surviving drafts and records trace several works in progress. These dates mark documented stages of the creative process, not necessarily when each story was first conceived.

  1. 26 August 2024

    In Love at 33 (working title)

    The novel is documented as already existing by this date.

  2. 13 September 2024

    Alexander and Livia (working title)

    An early record of another story project.

  3. 19–22 September 2024

    Four Worlds (working title)

    Records of the story involving Edith, followed by a preserved manuscript file.

  4. 25 October 2024

    Beautiful and Not So Beautiful → Unrecognisable

    An early version of the story, then centred on Ania and Marek. The completed novel now carries the publishing title Unrecognisable.

  5. 14 November 2024

    In Love at 33 (working title)

    The last recorded modification of a preserved version of the manuscript.

Note: All titles except Unrecognisable are provisional and may change before publication. The development timeline does not indicate publication dates.
